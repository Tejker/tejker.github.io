My research interests lie at the intersection of robotics, robot learning, autonomous systems, and data-driven engineering. I am particularly interested in combining geometric reasoning, learned dynamics, and physical-system understanding to develop more capable robotic systems.

## Research Projects

### World Models for Geometry-Aware Robot Manipulation

**Human-Interactive Robotics Lab (HiRo Lab), IISc** · *May–July 2026*

**Research focus:** Learning from Demonstration, trajectory retrieval, and model-based reasoning.

- Investigated Geometry-aware Policy Imitation (GPI) and identified policy-averaging failures in environments with homotopy ambiguity.
- Developed neural world models for one-step and direct multi-step dynamics prediction.
- Integrated predicted future rewards into GPI to support consequence-aware trajectory retrieval.
- Implemented closed-loop Panda manipulation in MuJoCo using inverse kinematics and SE(3) task-space control.

**Objective:** Investigate how predicted future consequences can help a robot distinguish between geometrically similar trajectories with different outcomes.

### Data-Driven Anomaly Detection for Telescope Systems

**ARIES, Nainital** · *IASc–INSA–NASI Summer Research Fellowship · May–July 2025*

**Research focus:** Predictive maintenance, time-series analysis, and structural vibration.

- Developed a hybrid anomaly-detection pipeline for the azimuth and altitude motors of the 3.6 m Devasthal Telescope.
- Combined Random Forest, Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM, and LSTM Autoencoder models with custom time-series features.
- Developed a Streamlit interface for anomaly classification and severity visualization.
- Performed FFT-based vibration analysis using accelerometer data and compared findings with ANSYS modal frequencies.
