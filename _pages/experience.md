My research experience spans robot learning, autonomous systems, predictive maintenance, and experimental engineering.

## Research Experience

### Human-Interactive Robotics Lab (HiRo Lab)

**Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru** · *May–July 2026*

*Research Intern · Under Prof. Ravi Prakash*

- **Robot learning and policy analysis:** Analyzed Geometry-aware Policy Imitation (GPI) and identified policy-averaging failures under homotopy ambiguity using geometric retrieval and vector-field analysis.
- **World models:** Developed one-step and direct multi-step dynamics prediction models, integrating predicted future rewards into GPI for consequence-aware trajectory retrieval. Achieved 100% success across randomized environments.
- **Robot manipulation:** Implemented closed-loop Panda manipulation in MuJoCo using Mink inverse kinematics, SE(3) task-space control, gripper actuation, trajectory recording, and GPI-based demonstration execution.
